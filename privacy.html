Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

About EverFrame Publisher

EverFrame Publisher is a content management application for preparing, reviewing, and publishing original AI-generated videos to supported social media platforms.

Information We Process

When a user authorizes a social media account, the application may process account identifiers, usernames, authorization tokens, video files, captions, hashtags, and publishing status information.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to connect authorized accounts, prepare content, upload approved videos, and track publication results.

Data Storage and Security

The application is designed to process content locally where possible. Account credentials and authorization tokens must be protected against unauthorized access.

Third-Party Platforms

Approved content and necessary account information are transmitted to connected platforms through their official APIs. Those platforms have their own privacy policies and data handling practices.

Data Retention and Deletion

Users may revoke platform access through their account settings. Requests concerning locally stored application data can be directed to the application operator.

Policy Changes

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in application functionality or data processing practices.

Contact

For privacy inquiries or data deletion requests, contact the application operator using the contact details provided in the developer application listing.
